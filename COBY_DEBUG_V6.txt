Do NOT mistake those historical core constants for the current Phase 6D gameplay rule.

Current Phase 6D traversal:
- movement envelope is expanded to whole world during active turn.
- walking retains terrain steepness/collision/void behavior through existing base movement.
- jump quota is bypassed by Phase 6D wrapper.
- jump cooldown 450 ms.
- successful jump visual motion shortened to 500 ms.
- projectile/special resolution still locks movement.
- existing movement wrappers still preserve touch-pickup collection.
