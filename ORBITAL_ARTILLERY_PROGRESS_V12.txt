V0.9 CLOSING GAMEPLAY RULE — PICKUP FRENZY
Early game:
- turns 1–9 retain the baseline pickup cadence: every 3 turns.

Late game:
- from turn 10 onward there is one pickup spawn opportunity every turn.
- maximum 4 live pickups on the map.
- pickup lifetime remains 4 turns.
- Phase 6E weighted pool remains 25 / 20 / 20 / 12 / 12 / 8 / 3.

Collection:
- each player may collect at most 1 pickup during their own turn.
- pickup collection is contact-only.
- collecting a pickup does not end the turn.
- a player may still move and fire after collecting it.
- explosions/projectiles no longer collect pickups.
- Nuke may still destroy pickups intersected by its beam.
- inventory remains Permanent Basic + 2 special slots.

PHASE 10 — SAVED BUT NOT STARTED
The large visual direction is intentionally preserved for later:
- animal characters mounted on small artillery vehicles as the preferred starting direction.
- current tank/cart placeholders will be replaced.
- selectable characters/vehicles can be explored.
- every map should gain a distinct visual identity, background and terrain theme.
- maps may gain upper/secondary terrain masses: cliffs, floating rocks, lunar formations, canyon structures, etc., not merely a flat second floor.
- access to elevated terrain should first be solved through map design.
- teleport/reposition utility remains an idea to evaluate only if vertical gameplay needs it.
- higher HP (150/175/200) remains a future balance experiment, not part of v0.9.

RELEASE INTERPRETATION
v0.9 Beta means the intended functional/mechanical game exists and is playable end-to-end. It does NOT mean final art or v1.0 visual identity.
