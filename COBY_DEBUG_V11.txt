CONFIRMED FINDING FIXED
A1 — PHASE 6F ACTIVE-WEAPON BADGE DID NOT FOLLOW THE VEHICLE'S VISUAL MOTION
Root cause:
- renderer6f.js positioned the badge from player.spawn.
- the base renderer positions an animated vehicle from its motion interpolation while jumping/falling/knockback motion is active.
- therefore the vehicle could visually travel through an animation while the badge snapped immediately to the authoritative destination spawn.

Impact:
- presentation-only desynchronization introduced by Phase 6F.
- gameplay/server authority was not affected.

Fix:
- renderer6f now calculates the same visual motion interpolation used by the base renderer for normal jump, void jump, generic fall and knockback/knockbackVoid motion.
- the active-weapon badge anchors to that interpolated visual position rather than raw spawn.
- frontend renderer cache advanced to phase6f-spectator-polish-2.
