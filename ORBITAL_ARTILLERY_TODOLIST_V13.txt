==================================================
These are not blockers to the Phase 9 code closure; they are the first things to observe naturally in the beta build after the final pickup pacing change.

[ ] Turn 10 starts the late-game pickup frenzy.
[ ] From turn 10 onward, at most one new pickup appears per turn.
[ ] No more than 4 live pickups are visible at once.
[ ] Expired pickups disappear normally and later turns can refill the map.
