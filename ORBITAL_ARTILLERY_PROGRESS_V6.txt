PHASE 6D — CURRENT NUKE DESIGN
Rarity:
- weight 3 in current pre-final pool total 126.

Targeting:
- normal authoritative artillery projectile acts as designator.
- angle/power/wind affect designator normally.
- designator impact locks Nuke target.
- all clients receive active aim state; spectators see the same live aim/trajectory produced by the active player.

Cinematic timing:
- warning after target lock: 3000 ms.
- sustained laser: 3000 ms.
- resolution/afterglow buffer: approximately 1500 ms after beam.

Visual presentation code:
- darkening warning phase.
- animated dashed lock/cut line.
- growing concentric target rings/countdown.
- multi-layer magenta/white beam with large glow.
- sustained particles/pulses/jitter during beam.
- full-screen energy tint.
- terrain-collapse afterglow before turn resolution.
- separate transparent overlay canvas remains used to avoid base-renderer erase races.

Terrain destruction:
- horizontal half-length 900 world units before edge clamping (~1800 total width).
- direct beam half-width 115.
- cut radius 145.
- cut depth 4200.
- cut step 105.
- purpose is to remove a major terrain section and make falls the primary strategic threat.

Damage/rules:
- 20 direct HP.
- full self/friendly damage.
- Shield halves direct hit and is consumed.
- no conventional knockback.
- terrain-caused falling is intentional.
- intersected pickups are destroyed, not collected.
- Nuke always ends offensive turn after resolution if match continues.
