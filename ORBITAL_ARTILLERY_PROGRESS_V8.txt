GAMEPLAY VALUES PRESERVED IN 6E
==================================================
Phase 6E intentionally does NOT change weapon damage/radii from the current implemented baseline.
It also preserves current pacing until manual play gives evidence to change it.

Pacing baseline:
- turn: 40 s
- pickup spawn cadence: every 3 turns
- pickup lifetime: 4 real turns
- max pickups on map: 2
- inventory: 2 special slots + permanent Basic
- free active-turn movement: yes
- per-turn jump quota: none
- normal jump cooldown: 450 ms
- normal jump visual: ~500 ms
- jump distance: 180
- move step: 15

Weapon baseline retained:
- Basic: 45 max damage, radius 260, crater 135/165
- Heavy: 60 max damage, radius 320, crater 190/90
- Triple: 3 shots ±6°, 20 each, radius 175, crater 78/78
- Cluster: 5 subimpacts, 14 each, radius 150, crater 70/70
- Shield: next applicable damage ×0.5
- Heal: +30, max HP 100
- Air Strike: 7 shells, 16 max damage/shell, radius 165, crater 72/62
- Nuke: 20 direct HP, no conventional knockback, catastrophic terrain removal

Manual gameplay may justify future tuning; do not silently change these values without recording the change.
