PHASE 6D — NUKE LASER IMPLEMENTATION
==================================================
Architecture:
- New backend wrapper module: phase6d.js.
- Phase 6D wraps Phase 6C rather than rewriting existing closed mechanics.
- server.js now uses Phase 6D public state, movement/jump/item/fire wrappers and turn advancement.
- /health reports phase 6D in current source.
- package CI checks phase6d.js.

Pickup rarity:
- Nuke weight: 3.
- Phase 6D extends the existing Phase 6C pool rather than replacing it.
- Effective pre-final-balance weights now:
  Heavy 30
  Triple 30
  Cluster 25
  Shield 15
  Heal 15
  Air Strike 8
  Nuke Laser 3
- Total weight: 126.
- Final rebalance remains deferred until manual multiplayer QA.

Targeting / timing:
- Nuke uses the normal authoritative artillery projectile as a TARGET DESIGNATOR.
- The designator follows the normal aim/power/wind trajectory.
- Its impact position locks the Nuke target.
- After target lock: 850 ms warning.
- Beam visible duration: approximately 420 ms.
- Resolution buffer follows beam presentation before turn/result progression.

Beam geometry:
- Diagonal beam centered on the locked target.
- Approximate horizontal half-length: 520 world units.
- Direct-hit half-width: 88 world units.
- Terrain strip is removed with a chain of deep overlapping cuts/craters.
- Cut step: 115.
- Cut radius: 92.
- Cut depth: 2450.

Player interaction:
- Direct beam damage: 20 HP.
- Full self damage.
- Full friendly fire.
- Shield halves the applicable direct hit and is consumed.
- No conventional Nuke knockback is applied.
- Terrain removal can still make players fall; this is intentional and is the main danger of the weapon.
- Players over destroyed void terrain receive fall presentation before void death finalization.

Pickup interaction:
- Pickups intersected by the beam are DESTROYED.
- They are NOT collected by the shooter.
- Nuke therefore does not use explosion-pickup-retains-turn behavior.

Turn behavior:
- Nuke always consumes the offensive action and ends the turn after its sequence resolves, unless match completion ends the match instead.
