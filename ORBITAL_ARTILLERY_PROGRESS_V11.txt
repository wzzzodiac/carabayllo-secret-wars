PHASE 7A.1 — MATCH STATS & EVENT TRACKING
New backend layer: phase7a1.js over the hardened Phase 6E gameplay stack.

Authoritative per-player stats:
- damage dealt
- damage received
- kills
- assists
- deaths
- self-KOs
- void deaths
- pickups collected
- biggest hit
- successful weapon/utility uses
- per-weapon usage counts

Rules:
- self-damage counts as damage received, not damage dealt.
- environmental void HP removal does not become fake damage.
- if a real impact deals damage and the same resolution also causes a void death, only the real lastDamage amount is credited.
- kill attribution supports player, self-KO, void and shooter-caused terrain collapse.
- assists: every other previous non-self contributor to the victim in the current match receives one assist when another player gets the kill.

Authoritative event feed:
- fire
- damage
- self-damage
- death / cause
- pickup
- Shield/Heal/other utility usage
- rematch
- retained server-side up to 36 recent events.

End-of-match summary:
- winner/result
- duration
- number of turns
- top damage player
- top kills player
- most-used weapon.

Frontend:
- match-stats7a1.js provides a live DOM scoreboard below the battle layout.
- columns: damage, kills, assists, damage taken, pickups, uses, biggest hit.
- recent match event feed.
- final match summary.
- frontend panel does not alter Canvas/camera/game authority.
